# Costs and history

This page is about what an author pays for, where it is shown in the author app, and where to see what happened to your materials.

## What an author pays for {#what-you-pay}

Course Supporter provides two software services: homework review and course material processing. You pay with prepaid packages. The platform does not sell access to courses and does not charge for it. A student pays for the review of their own work, and an author pays for processing the materials of their course. One package works for both services.

An author pays with units from their package for three things:

- **Material processing.** The platform turns every uploaded material into text and analyses it, and that costs money. Repeated processing that you started yourself is paid again; repeated processing on the platform's initiative is not. More — in the section [Who pays for processing](materials.md#cost).
- **The Methodist's work.** Writing section descriptions also uses units, so run the Methodist when the course has been uploaded ([Section descriptions: the Methodist](methodist.md#when)).
- **Preparing an assignment for review.** The platform does this before the first review of an assignment and again after the assignment is changed. In the costs it is a separate column, "Task preparation (Підготовка завдань)"; it does not concern the student. A preparation started by a student's submission is not stopped when you are short of units.

A student pays for the review of their own work. Tests are checked without using units as long as the student has something left in a package.

If you want to pay for the reviews of the students of your course, or of one student, yourself, arrange it with the platform administrator: for now, only they can set this up ([how to contact them](../index.md#authors)).

If you pay for students' reviews, they wait in line when your package does not have enough units, and they start by themselves after you buy a package: a message about it is shown under the header of the app. The student sees "Waiting for review (Чекає перевірки)" in the meantime.

## Package and units {#package}

A unit is not money and not a means of payment, but an amount of prepaid work by the platform. Units are whole numbers only.

- You have one amount of units for all your courses. Each purchase is a separate package; valid packages add up together. The author's package and students' packages are separate: units are not transferred between them.
- Units are valid only for the platform's two services. They cannot be transferred to another account and are not exchanged for money.
- A package is valid for six months from the date of purchase; the unused remaining units are not carried over after that. Units are used first from the package whose term ends the earliest.
- You cannot add units to an existing package: you buy a new package. Packages have a fixed size; you cannot buy an arbitrary number of units on top.
- For the unused part of a valid package, we refund the payment — [Refunds](https://pythoncourse.me/en/refunds/). To ask for a refund for unused units, write to support@pythoncourse.me; how to do it and what to include — on the [Refunds](https://pythoncourse.me/en/refunds/) page. While the request is being handled, these units are shown among your remaining units as "Temporarily unavailable (Тимчасово недоступно)".

You buy a package in the author app, in the "Units (Юніти)" section: choose a package, confirm the terms — once, and again whenever the terms are updated — and pay on the checkout page. After payment, the checkout page takes you back to "Units", and the app shows whether the units have arrived.

The "Units (Юніти)" page has three blocks. "Remaining units (Залишок)" — how many units are available, how many are reserved for actions in progress, and when some units are about to expire. "Packages (Пакети)" — what you can buy: the size of a package in units, the price in euros excluding taxes (the final amount is shown on the checkout page before payment), the validity period. "Unit movements (Рухи юнітів)" — every arrival and charge with a note of what it was: buying a package, processing materials, preparing an assignment, reviewing work, a payment refund, the end of a package's validity. After payment the app checks by itself whether the units have arrived: first you see "Adding… (Зараховуємо…)", then how many units were added and how many are now available.

The detailed payment rules are in the platform's [Terms of Use](https://pythoncourse.me/en/terms/) and on the [pricing page](https://pythoncourse.me/en/pricing/).

## Where costs are shown {#where}

Costs are shown on two pages of the author app; their names are in the header.

| Page | What is on it |
|---|---|
| "Costs (Витрати)" | the units charged to your package in the chosen month (arrows next to the month name): the line "Spent in … (Витрачено за …)" and a table by course — "Material processing (Обробка матеріалів)", "Task preparation (Підготовка завдань)", "Work reviews (Перевірки робіт)", "Total spent (Разом витрачено)", "Covered (Покрито)" |
| "Homework costs (Витрати ДЗ)" (on the page itself — "Homework review costs (Витрати на перевірку ДЗ)") | the same costs by assignment: a course expands to show assignments with the columns "Reviews (Перевірок)", "Spent (Витрачено)" and "Covered (Покрито)". Only the units charged to your package are here (task preparation and the reviews you pay for under an arrangement); students' own units are not shown |

All amounts on these pages are shown in units, the way you pay. "Covered (Покрито)" means units that the platform took on or that are covered by an arrangement: they were not charged to your package. These pages have no overspending warning, but on the "Units (Юніти)" and "My courses (Мої курси)" pages the app reminds you when, at your pace, your units will last only a short time and when a package is about to expire; and if reviews of your students' work that you pay for are waiting for units, a message about it appears under the header of every page.

Before an action that costs units starts, the app shows its estimated cost — "about …" — and how many units you have available. This is the upload window (for each file separately), confirming the file roles of a material with code, checking a test with the model and publishing a test. For repeated processing from a material's card or from the section menu the app shows no number.

While an action runs, the platform reserves part of your available units — on the "Units (Юніти)" page it is shown as the line "Reserved for actions in progress (Зарезервовано для дій, які виконуються зараз)". When the action finishes, its actual cost is charged and the rest of the reserve returns to the package. If the action fails, the whole reserve returns and nothing is charged.

If your available units are not enough, the action does not start. In the windows that show the cost, instead of the start you see "This action costs about …, and you have …. You are … short." (Ця дія коштує приблизно …, у вас …. Не вистачає …) and the link "Buy units (Купити юніти)"; after buying, go back to that tab and start the action again. If the units ran out only when processing of a material was starting, the material is kept, but it shows "Error (Помилка)" and the message "Processing has not started: there were not enough units for it (Обробку не почато: на неї не вистачило юнітів…)". Buy a package in the "Units (Юніти)" section and press "Retry processing (Повторити обробку)".

## Materials history {#history}

The "Materials history (Історія матеріалів)" page answers the question of what happened to your materials. Its subtitle is "What happened to your materials — newest first" («Що відбувалося з вашими матеріалами — від найновішого»). The table has the columns "Material (Матеріал)", "State (Стан)", "Last job (Остання робота)", "Jobs (Робіт)" and "Activity (Активність)".

<!-- frame 14: the "Materials history (Історія матеріалів)" page with several rows -->

- It shows material processing, preparing materials with code for confirming the files' roles, and preparing a project's starter archive.
- The Methodist's work and tests are not shown here. While the Methodist is working, you can see it in the "Recent jobs (Останні роботи)" strip in the app header.
- A deleted material stays in the history marked "Material deleted (Матеріал видалено)". Who deleted it — you or the security check — the app does not distinguish (see [The material did not pass the security check](materials.md#safety)).

## If a charge seems wrong {#questions}

First look at "Unit movements (Рухи юнітів)" on the "Units (Юніти)" page: it shows every charge, its kind and its date. Write to the Course Supporter platform administrator ([how to contact them](../index.md#authors)): name the course, the material and the time. By decision of support, units may be returned to the package.
