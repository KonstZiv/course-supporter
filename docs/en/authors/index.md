# For authors

This section is for course authors: how to prepare a course on the Course Supporter platform for students to work in, and what to do next.

The author app has an English version: switch it with the "English" button in the app header or on the sign-in page. When this guide names a button or a screen, it gives the English name first and, in brackets, the Ukrainian one — for example, "Save (Зберегти)" — so you can find it whichever language your app is in.

## What the platform does for you {#what}

The Course Supporter platform takes your learning materials and prepares the course for students to work in. Two programs help it do this — the Methodist and the Mentor.

**The Methodist** reads the materials of each section of the course and writes its description: what the section teaches, what the student has to show in their homework, which mistakes are the most common. Along the way, it leaves notes for you — including on gaps and inconsistencies it noticed in the materials. You start the Methodist yourself, and you can correct every description it writes.

**The Mentor** automatically reviews students' homework; no person takes part in grading. It relies on the assignment text, the list of criteria for it, the description of the section with the assignment and the course description. The more accurate the descriptions, the more accurate the review.

Tests are checked by the system itself — against the correct answers you marked. The student gets a score straight away, without any person involved; under wrong answers they see an explanation, if one is already there.

Which assistant reads what and how the platform processes materials — on the page [How the platform understands your course](how-it-works.md).

## The author's path, step by step {#steps}

1. Sign in to the author app with your access key — [Signing in and your first course](first-course.md).
2. Create a course and set its language — same page.
3. Create the sections of the course — straight away in the order the student should see them: [Course structure](structure.md).
4. Upload materials into each section: educational ones for students, methodological ones for the platform's assistants: [Materials](materials.md).
5. Add assignments and tests: [Assignments](tasks.md), [Tests](tests.md).
6. When everything is uploaded, run the Methodist for the whole course — [Section descriptions: the Methodist](methodist.md#when).
7. Review the section descriptions, correct what is needed and approve them — [same page](methodist.md#edit).
8. Add students and enrol them in the course — [Students and enrolment](students.md).
9. Keep an eye on your units and costs — the "Units (Юніти)" and "Costs (Витрати)" pages in the app; details — [Costs and history](costs-history.md).

Two rules of order will save you time and money.

**First all materials and tests, then the Methodist, then editing the descriptions.** A material or test added after the Methodist marks the section description as outdated, and running the Methodist again overwrites your edits in that section. The Methodist does not read the tests themselves, but a new test also makes the description outdated.

**Get the descriptions of sections with assignments right before the first piece of work is submitted.** An assignment's list of criteria is put together with the first submitted piece of work, and a later edit of the section description no longer reaches it.
