# Students' work and reviews

For each student, the author app shows everything they have submitted and the reviews of it — the same ones the student received, plus a breakdown by criteria. This way you see where a student stumbles and how the Mentor assesses work on your assignments.

To open it: "Students (Студенти)" in the app header → "Submissions (Здачі)" in the student's row.

## All of a student's submissions {#list}

At the top — the student's name and login and the "Enrolled in: (Записаний на курси:)" line with the date of enrolment in each course. The "All courses (Усі курси)" switch narrows the list to one course.

The table has every piece of work submitted, newest first: "Assignment (Завдання)", "Course (Курс)", "Attempt (Спроба)" (which one it is), "Submitted (Здано)", "Review (Рецензія)" (when it was written), "Score (Бал)" and "Pass (Залік)" ("Yes (Так)" or "No (Ні)"). Older submissions may have no attempt number — then there is "—".

If there is no review yet, the "Review" column says why: "being checked (перевіряється)", "waiting for the student's units (чекає юнітів студента)", "waiting for your units (чекає ваших юнітів)" (when you pay for this student's reviews), "the file could not be opened (файл не вдалося відкрити)" or "does not look like an attempt (не схоже на спробу)".

Next to an assignment's name there may be marks: "resubmitted unchanged (повтор без змін)" — the student submitted the same work again and got a copy of the previous review ([Assignments](tasks.md#review)); "rejected (відхилено)" — the work was not accepted for review.

Click a row to open that submission.

## One submission {#submission}

At the top — the assignment and the course, the student, the attempt number, when the work was submitted and when the review was written, and the language of the review. Next to them — buttons:

- **"Download the work (Завантажити роботу)"** — the file or archive the student submitted (there is no such button for tests);
- **"Assignment criteria (Критерії завдання)"** — the list of criteria the Mentor reviews this assignment against;
- **"Previous attempt (Попередня спроба)"** — if the student has already submitted this assignment.

Then — "Score (Бал)" and "Passed (Зараховано)" or "Not passed (Не зараховано)", and below them — the rule by which the pass was decided: the score threshold and, if you marked criteria as mandatory for passing, how many of them. Work reviewed long ago may not have the line with the rule. How the pass is decided — [Assignments](tasks.md#review).

### Criteria breakdown {#criteria}

For each criterion of the assignment:

- the weight — "major (головний)", "moderate (бажаний)" or "minor (додатковий)" (small requirements are marked "point (пункт)") and the "Mandatory for passing (Обов'язковий для заліку)" mark, if you set it;
- the Mentor's verdict — "Met (Виконано)", "Not met (Не виконано)" or "Not confirmed (Не підтверджено)";
- "Quote from the work (Цитата з роботи)" — the place in the work the Mentor relied on, and "What is missing (Чого бракує)" if the criterion is not met;
- for repeated attempts — what became of the remark: "new remark (нове зауваження)", "still open from the last attempt (лишилось з минулої спроби)", "fixed (виправлено)" or "broke after being fixed (зламалося після виправлення)".

### The review {#review}

Below is the text of the review itself, as the student received it. If the student rated the review, you will see "The student marked: the review helped." («Студент позначив: рецензія допомогла.») or "…did not help." («…не допомогла.»)

While students cannot write replies to a review, this page has no conversation block.
