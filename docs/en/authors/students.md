# Students and enrolment

Students take your course in the learning portal. There are two ways to bring them there: open enrolment by link — then students enrol themselves — or add each student by hand on the "Students (Студенти)" page. What this looks like for a student — on the page [Joining a course and signing in](../students/join.md).

## Enrolment by link {#open}

On the course page, in the top bar, there is the "Enrolment by link (Запис за посиланням)" button with a state mark — "open (відкрито)" or "closed (закрито)".

1. Press "Enrolment by link", and in the window — "Open enrolment (Відкрити запис)".
2. The "Course link (Посилання на курс)" appears in the window. Press "Copy (Копіювати)" and give the link to students — in a mailing, on a website or in a chat.

What you need to know:

- Students enrol by this link without your approval.
- The course is free for them; they pay for the reviews of their work with units themselves.
- Only a student with a confirmed email can enrol: the student gets onto the course as soon as they confirm their email with the link in the email.
- On the "Students" page, such a student has the "self-enrolled (записався сам)" mark next to their name.

In the same window you can:

- **"Issue a new link (Випустити нове посилання)"** — if the old one got to the wrong place. The old link stops working; those who have already enrolled stay on the course.
- **"Close enrolment (Закрити запис)"** — new students will not be able to enrol; those who have already enrolled stay on the course. You can open enrolment again with the same button.

The app asks you to confirm both steps before doing them.

## The "Students" page {#list}

In the app header there is the "Students (Студенти)" item. Here are all your students: name, login, portal access status ("Active (Активний)", "Revoked (Відкликано)" or "No portal access (Немає доступу до порталу)"), number of courses and actions. The page needs a wide screen: on a phone or in a narrow window, the app asks you to make the window wider.

Actions in a student's row:

- **"Submissions (Здачі)"** — all the work the student has submitted and the reviews of it.
- **"Revoke (Відкликати)"** — close the student's access to the portal. The student and their whole history are kept; you can give the access back with the "Restore (Відновити)" button.
- **"Reset password (Скинути пароль)"** — set a new portal password for the student. The access does not change — only the password is updated; tell the student the new password yourself.

To see which courses a student is enrolled in, press the arrow next to their name. There you also find "Add a course (Додати курс)" and "Remove from the course (Відрахувати з курсу)".

## Adding a student by hand {#add}

This is convenient when there are few students or you want to decide yourself who is on the course.

1. On the "Students" page, press "Add a student (Додати студента)", choose "New student (Новий студент)", enter the display name, login and password and press "Create (Створити)".
2. In the new student's row, press the arrow, then "Add a course", and choose the course.
3. Press "Copy the portal link (Скопіювати посилання порталу)" and send the student this link together with the login and password. With this link the student signs in to your portal.

If the student already has their own identifier in your learning system, choose "New student with your own ID (Новий студент із власним ID)" and enter this identifier. To give a portal sign-in to a student who is already on the platform — "Give access to an existing student (Дати доступ наявному студенту)".
