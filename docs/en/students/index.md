# For students

## A test in the portal {#test}

You take a test right in the portal — there is no file to send. A test appears in the course when the
author publishes it; before that, it is not visible in the course.

- Open the assignment: the portal shows the test itself — the questions and answer options.
- There is a checkbox next to each option in every question. Mark all the options you think are
  correct. If a question has several correct ones, the question text says so.
- A question without a single mark counts as a wrong answer.
- When everything is ready, send all the answers together — with one press.

A question counts only when exactly all the correct options are marked: an extra marked option, just
like a missed one, makes the answer wrong.

Sometimes a test temporarily does not accept answers. Then you will not be able to send your answers;
come back to the test later.

If the test was updated just while you were answering, answers to the old version are not accepted:
re-read the test and answer the new version.

## The review {#review}

The review of a test arrives straight away: the system puts it together against the correct answers
set by the author, without any person involved.

- **At the top — the verdict and the score.** The score is the share of questions you answered
  correctly, as a percentage, rounded down to a whole number; every question weighs the same and
  counts only when exactly all the correct options are marked. The verdict — "Passed" or "Not passed"
  — appears when the author has set a pass mark: the test is passed if the score is not lower than the
  pass mark. More — in the section [How the score is calculated](../authors/tests.md#score).
- **Then — each question:** "Correct" or "Incorrect".
- **For a wrong answer**, the review adds the correct option — as it is written in the test — and an
  explanation of why exactly it is correct. Sometimes there is no explanation; then the review says
  so.
- **At the end**, if the test is not passed, — an offer to take it again.

If you read the review in a language other than the course language and there are no explanations in
your language yet, the review shows them in the course language and says so. After the first such
review, the system orders explanations in your language, so in your next attempts they will usually be
there already.

## Attempts {#attempts}

You can make as many attempts as you like. Each attempt is checked separately, and each has its own
review — even if you sent the same answers again.

If an attempt could not be checked, the portal tells you — send your answers again.

## Units and paying for a review {#units}

A package of units pays for the review of homework. You can buy it in the student portal, in the "Units" section: choose a package, confirm the terms before your first purchase and pay on the checkout page. A unit is not money and not a means of payment, but an amount of the platform's service. The "Units" page shows how many units are available, when some of them will expire soon and what has happened to them.

Before you submit work, the portal shows the estimated cost of the review and how many units you have available. Tests do not use up units as long as something remains in your package; when there are no units at all, a test cannot be submitted — buy a package first. If you submit the same work again and ask for the review in the same language, and neither the assignment nor the Mentor has changed in the meantime, there is no new review: you get a copy of the previous review, and no units are used up. If you ask for the review in another language, or the Mentor has been updated in the meantime, the same work is reviewed again, as a new one, and units are used up. Reviewing projects does not use up units for now.

If you do not have enough units, instead of the submit button the portal shows "Not enough units (Не вистачає юнітів)" and the link "Buy units (Купити юніти)". The chosen file stays on the page: after buying, go back to this tab — the portal notices by itself that you have enough units. If the work has already been accepted but its review has not started because of a shortage of units, it is marked "Paused (Призупинено)": after you buy a package the review continues by itself, and you do not have to submit the work again.

If the author of the course pays for the review of your work, the portal says "The course author pays for the review of this work (Перевірку цієї роботи оплачує автор курсу)", and there is no "Units" item in the menu. If the author does not have enough units, the work is still accepted but it waits: "Waiting for review (Чекає перевірки)" — the review starts when the author buys units. You do not have to submit the work again.

When you are running low on units, the portal reminds you on the "My courses" and "Units" pages and tells you roughly how many reviews they will cover; it also reminds you when some units are about to expire: a package is valid for six months from the day of purchase.

## Replies to a review {#replies}

**Turned off for now.** This feature will be turned on together with answers to replies in the review.

You can disagree with a comment in the review, ask about it or add something: below the comment you
write a reply — a **question**, an **objection** or a **comment**. The next review of your work will
answer every reply.

- **The answer comes with your next submission.** Send your work again — corrected or the same: the
  review of it will answer your replies. If the replies concern the work, the same work is reviewed
  anew; for replies not about the work, the answer is short, without a new review.
- **You can write only to the latest review.** While a new submission is being reviewed, replies to
  the previous review are not accepted — wait for the new one and write to it. If the author changed
  the assignment after the review, replies to it are not accepted either.
- **Up to 5 replies per comment** between two submissions, each up to 2,000 characters.
- **Only the work affects the grade.** A reply does not change the verdict by itself: for the grade to
  change, the work has to change. Requests to change the grade or instructions to the review system in
  a reply do not change the grade.

Replies and the answers to them are kept in threads: each comment has its own, and it runs through all
your attempts as long as the assignment has not changed.
