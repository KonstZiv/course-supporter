# Joining a course and signing in

The Course Supporter learning portal is where you take a course: you read the materials, submit homework and get reviews. There are two ways into the portal: join a course by a link from the author, or sign in with the login and password the author created for you.

## Joining by a link from the author {#join}

1. Open the link for joining the course that the author gave you. The "Join the course" page opens: it shows the course title, the author, the course languages and the payment terms — for example, "The course is free; reviews of your work are paid for in units." The "Prices" link leads to the page with prices.
2. Fill in the "Create your portal sign-in" form: name (optional), login, email, password and interface language. Tick "I am 18 or older." and press "Join".
3. The portal shows "Confirm your email", and an email with a link arrives at the address you gave. Open the link from the email within 7 days and press "Confirm email".
4. Joining is complete: the portal lets you straight in and opens the course page. Next time, sign in on the "Sign in" page (see [Signing in to the portal](#sign-in)).

If you do not confirm your email within 7 days, the enrolment is cancelled — join again by the same link.

If the email has not arrived, check your "Spam" folder, then press "Send the email again" (the button becomes available after a few seconds). After that, the link from the previous email no longer works.

If someone took your login while you were confirming your email, the portal asks you for a new login — enter it and press "Save and confirm"; your request to join is kept.

If the page says "The enrollment link is invalid or enrollment is closed", ask the author for a new link.

## If you already have a sign-in with this author's portal {#existing}

Do not create a new sign-in: below the form, press "Sign in" and sign in. Then the joining page offers to join in one step — the "Join the course" button.

This needs a confirmed email. If your sign-in has no email yet, add it on the portal's home page, confirm it with the link in the email and open the joining link again.

## Signing in to the portal {#sign-in}

Each author's portal has its own address, and you have to sign in exactly there — you cannot sign in on the campus.pythoncourse.me home page. Save the address of the sign-in page: it is where you sign in to the portal from any device.

- **With login and password.** On the "Sign in" page, enter your login and password — the ones you created when joining, or the ones the author gave you.
- **With email.** Press "Sign in with email" and enter your confirmed email and your portal password. If this password fits several sign-ins, the portal asks you to choose the author and the login.
- **Forgot your password?** A link with this name is on the "Sign in" page.

You can change the portal's interface language at any time — with the language switcher in the portal header.

## If the author added you themselves {#added-by-author}

The author can create a sign-in for you themselves — then they send you the address of the sign-in page, your login and password. You do not need to join by a link: just sign in on the "Sign in" page.
